Judge only what the words "{{NODE}}" mean, not whether the event happened in this

Group events only if they are the same kind of event at a high level and work the same
way: they lead to the same kinds of outcomes (Beckers and Halpern, "Abstracting Causal
Models"). An event and its opposite describe one thing with opposite values: one
happening means the other did not (e.g. "paying rent" and "falling behind on rent";
"keeping the flat" and "eviction"). An event that prevents, stops, causes or follows
the group is not its opposite: it is a different event (e.g. "a relative paying the
rent" prevents "eviction" but is a different event).
